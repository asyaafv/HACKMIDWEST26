# Gate

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User-approved React, TypeScript, and Vite. Typed asynchronous local demo service, replaceable by a backend adapter.

## Users
Agent operators and finance reviewers inspecting proposed company spending, its business evidence, and policy decisions.

## Product Purpose
Make every agent transaction and its reason inspectable in a working hackathon frontend demo.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Seven pages: Overview, Needs a decision, Transactions/Requests, Gatekeepers, Policies, Audit log, Settings. Overview is the home screen; the focused decision inbox includes all pending requests, ordered by priority then oldest first. Local request creation and decisions, Gatekeeper management, editable future-evaluation policies, and versioned persistence. No backend calls or financial execution. The repository's broader backend plan is context, not this frontend's implementation scope.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Gate. Supplied rainbow logo only in the top-left brand. User-supplied dark dashboard is visual authority. Agents navigation/page renamed Gatekeepers. Supplied original ten character images used as circular crops; images may be reused. Original artwork stays intact; user-authorized clean plates repair eye regions for character animation. Unapproved visual changes require the user's decision.

## Evidence on Hand
Reference screenshot and original logo PNG supplied in chat; ten original character PNGs supplied in a ZIP. User-authorized merchant logos are fetched from official company websites and stored locally with source provenance; generated substitutes are not authorized. Demo records are synthetic, with 24 initial records and consistent dates.

## Product Principles
- Preserve the approved screenshot and artwork.
- Keep decisions and their evidence inspectable.
- Derive counts and activity from one shared state.
- Persist only after storage succeeds; failures are visible.
- Keep the demo boundary visible.
